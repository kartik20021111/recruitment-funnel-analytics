# Interview Prep
*Talking points drawn directly from this project.*
*Use these when asked "tell me about a project" or 
"how do you approach HR analytics."*

---

## The Headline Story (30-second version)
"I built a recruitment funnel analytics project from scratch —
designed the data architecture, generated 1,000 rows of 
realistic mock data, built a 5-sheet Excel analytics workbook,
and documented the entire methodology in a linked knowledge base.
The project surface areas include funnel conversion, SLA compliance,
channel ROI, and time-to-fill analysis — the four metrics that
HR leadership actually asks about in QBRs."

---

## Analytical Decisions to Mention (shows depth)
1. Separating Funnel Stage Reached from Current Stage
   — and why mixing them is the most common dashboard mistake
2. Why NETWORKDAYS matters for SLA tracking
3. Why Time to Fill ≠ Time to Hire
4. Why Employee Referral conversion rate matters more than volume
5. Why 4 SLAs instead of 1 aggregate SLA preserves accountability

---

## The On Hold Insight (shows you can see invisible problems)
"One of the most interesting findings was 139 candidates sitting
On Hold — nearly 3.5x the number who actually joined.
In most companies this population is invisible in reports.
I flagged it as a structural risk: candidates aging in the
pipeline with no communication SLA, and a systemic indicator
of hiring manager indecision or budget uncertainty."
