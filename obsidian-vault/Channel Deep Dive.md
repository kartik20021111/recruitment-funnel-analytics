## The Quality vs Volume Trap
LinkedIn has the highest application volume (283) but 3rd best 
conversion. The trap: optimizing for volume makes LinkedIn 
look like the best channel. Optimizing for conversion reveals 
it is a mid-tier channel being over-resourced.

## Referral Economics
Referral fee: typically ₹5,000–15,000 incentive per successful hire
vs Agency fee: ₹3–4.5 lakhs per hire at 15–18% of CTC
Referral hires also have documented 40–50% lower 90-day attrition
in most MNC HR studies.
→ [[Insights Log]]

## Campus as a Separate Category
Campus should not be compared directly with other channels because:
- Different timeline (months between offer and joining)
- Different candidate profile (freshers vs experienced)
- Different attrition risk (no-shows at joining are 2–3x higher)
Campus needs its own funnel, its own SLAs, and its own 
pre-boarding engagement calendar.
→ [[Insights Log]]

## Agency Restriction Framework
Recommended policy: Agency can only be engaged when:
- Role has been open >45 calendar days
- Zero shortlists via LinkedIn, Job Board, or Referral
- Role is specialist/senior (band 4+)
This prevents agency from becoming the default channel for
everything that looks difficult — which is how most MNCs
end up with 40% of hires via agency at premium cost.
