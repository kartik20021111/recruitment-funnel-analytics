# Real World Context

## Why This Project Exists

Most analytics projects are built to demonstrate tools.
This one was built to understand gaps.

The specific gap: what does people analytics actually surface 
in an organisation that standard HR reporting misses?
You cannot learn this from a course.
You learn it by building the infrastructure yourself and 
asking what questions it can and cannot answer.

## What Organisations Actually Track vs What They Should

What most orgs track:
- Number of hires per quarter
- Time to hire as a single average number
- LinkedIn as the primary sourcing channel
- Rejection count as a compliance metric

What this project surfaces that most orgs miss:
- The On Hold pipeline as a hidden risk pool
- Time to Fill vs Time to Hire gap as a planning indicator
- SLA compliance by stage with different owners for each
- Offer decline reasons as a speed and engagement signal
- Channel conversion rate vs channel volume as separate metrics
- Budget Frozen rejections as a planning failure, not a recruitment failure

## The Methodology Gap in Real Orgs

Most HR teams receive reports. They do not design them.
The person who designs the report decides which questions 
the report can answer. If Time to Fill is not in the data model,
no one can ever ask that question from the report.

This project was built by someone who designed the data model — 
not just consumed the output. That design experience is what 
makes the difference between an HR analyst who reads dashboards 
and one who builds them.

## What I Would Look for When Joining an Org

1. Do they track Funnel Stage Reached separately from Current Stage?
   If not, their funnel numbers are wrong.
   
2. Do they measure Time to Fill or only Time to Hire?
   If only TTH, they cannot see where the process starts breaking.
   
3. Do they have SLA compliance by stage with named owners?
   If not, accountability for delays does not exist.
   
4. How is On Hold handled in their reporting?
   If it is counted as active pipeline, their health metrics are inflated.
   
5. What happens to offer decline data?
   If it disappears into a dropdown that no one analyses, 
   they are losing candidates and do not know why.

These five questions told in an interview signal you understand 
what mature people analytics looks like in practice.
